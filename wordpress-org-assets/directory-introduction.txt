BoltUtil is a USDT and USDC payment gateway for WooCommerce, connecting your WordPress store to the BoltUtil stablecoin payment service. Customers select a token and network, place an order, and continue to BoltUtil Hosted Checkout. They can use wallet payment or manual transfer. BoltUtil displays the receiving address and exact payable amount, monitors the network, and reports the confirmed result to WooCommerce. The plugin does not receive cryptocurrency or store wallet private keys or recovery phrases.

**Stablecoin payments for WooCommerce**

* Use a unified BoltUtil card with a USDT/USDC switch in invoice payments, Classic Checkout and Checkout Blocks.
* Accept USDT (Tether) and USDC payments on eligible merchant-enabled routes.
* Offer connected-wallet checkout with the address and exact amount filled in, or manual transfer with a QR code and receiving address.
* Support six networks: TRON (TRC20), Ethereum (ERC20), BNB Smart Chain (BEP20), Polygon PoS, Solana and Base. USDT has six routes and USDC five; available options depend on the merchant and service configuration.
* Receive payments directly in your configured wallet. The plugin does not hold customer funds or wallet private keys.
* Update WooCommerce orders after verified payment status and signed Webhooks; support merchant-scoped status rechecks.
* Integrate with Classic Checkout, Cart and Checkout Blocks, and WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS).
